The short version

Beehive Wire is a page of headlines that link to other publishers. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no comment box and no forms. We do not ask for, collect or sell personal information.

What the site records

Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not track readers from site to site. It reports totals such as page views, the referring site and the country a visit came from.

If you switch the page to dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser so the page remembers it. It never leaves your device.

Links to other sites

Every headline leaves this site for the publisher that reported it. What those sites collect is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

The YouTube videos

A small program posts a video of each edition to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube API. It has access to that one channel, granted by its owner, and to no one else's Google account or data. It collects nothing about viewers. YouTube's own handling of viewers is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
